# Episode 01 - Iron Rain

![Episode key frame](./data/header.png)

Rain starts at noon and every metal roof in Red Marsh begins to sing.

Riven meets Saya at a broken tram station while the crowd hides under welding tarps. They barter information for safe passage, but both are lying.

- Opening: wide overhead of flooded alleys
- Middle: tight dialogue under tram sparks
- End: sprint sequence through half-lit tunnel
