# Red Marsh District

![Aerial sketch of the district](./data/header.png)

The old industrial ring collapsed and flooded, creating red algae channels around rust towers.

## Atmosphere

- Fog hangs below bridge level
- Sirens replace church bells
- Street markets are active after dusk

### Travel Rule

Crossing bridges after midnight requires a blood-seal token.
