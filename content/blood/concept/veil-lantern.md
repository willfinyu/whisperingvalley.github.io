# Veil Lantern

![Prop sheet](./data/123.png)

The lantern stores short-lived shadows that can be projected as false paths.

## Mechanics

- Charge capacity: 4 echoes
- Cooldown: 20 heartbeats
- Failure state: burns a black ring into nearby walls

Used by scouts to pull patrol routes away from extraction teams.
